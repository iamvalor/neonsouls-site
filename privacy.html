Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains what data Neon Souls (the "app") collects, why, and how it's used. Neon Souls is developed by an independent developer, contactable at xva1our@gmail.com.

Account sign-in

Signing in is optional and only used to sync your save progress across devices. You can play entirely as a guest with no account at all — progress then stays only on your device. If you choose to sign in, one of the following providers is used, and only the provider you pick is contacted:

ProviderData receivedUsed for
Google (Sign-in with Google) Account ID, name, email, profile photo; access to an app-private folder in your Google Drive ("App Data" — not your regular Drive files) Identifying your account and storing your save/progress backup in that private Drive folder
Samsung Account Account ID, name, email, profile photo Identifying your account for cross-device progress sync
VK ID Account ID, name, email, profile photo Identifying your account for cross-device progress sync

Save data and progress backup

Your run history, meta-progression (unlocked upgrades, currencies) and settings are stored locally on your device. If you sign in with an account above, or in some cases even as a guest, a backup of this progress is also stored with our backend service provider, PlayFab (a Microsoft Azure service), identified by your account ID or an anonymous device-generated ID. This backup exists solely to let you restore progress on a new device or after reinstalling.

Crash and diagnostic data

The app may collect basic, anonymized crash and error diagnostics (e.g. stack traces) to help us find and fix bugs. This does not include personal information beyond what's technically necessary to diagnose the crash.

What we don't do

  • No advertising or ad-tracking SDKs
  • No selling or renting your data to third parties
  • No access to your Google Drive files outside the app's own private data folder

Your choices

You can sign out at any time from the in-game cloud save settings, which stops further syncing. To request deletion of your account-linked backup data, contact xva1our@gmail.com with your account provider and, if available, your in-game backup code.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
